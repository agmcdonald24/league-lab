### Iteration 8 — projection v2 (Andrew's model)

What exists: `mart_player_week_features` (as-of, no peeking), the OLS baseline, the backtest harness.
Note on expected points: `points_expected` is **not** a Sleeper projection — it is the ffverse
model's value of the opportunities a player *actually got* (targets, carries, their depth and field
position), so `diff_per_game` is "did he beat what his touches were worth", a retrospective. The
projection that looks forward is the Rankings baseline, which already uses target share, matchups
and Vegas. Iteration 8 replaces the baseline's core with a learned model and keeps the baseline as
the fallback the model must beat.
